# Noora Medical Research Instructions

## Overview
This skill contains a comprehensive collection of structured medical writing prompts, guidelines, and templates developed by Noora. These prompts are designed to guide agentic workflows for academic medical research, thesis writing, manuscript drafting, results extraction, and clinical statistical validation.
